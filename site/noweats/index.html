About

Noweats scrapes Twitter for tracks involving eating in the NYC area. It uses the Natural Language Toolkit to analyze tweet data and extract noun phrases that indicate what people are eating. Each hour it aggregates the extracted noun phrases into a table and also extracts some interesting food noun phrases to display.

The site owner is not responsible for any vulgar or offensive content appearing in the results. This content is scraped directly from Twitter, and no NLP technique is capable of filtering all such content since machines cannot understand natural language.

For source code, please visit the noweats github.
